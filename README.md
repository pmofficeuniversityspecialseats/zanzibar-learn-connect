# Zanzibar Students Connect

Create a modern, professional and fully responsive official government-style website for:



OFISI YA MBUNGE WA VYUO NA VYUO VIKUU ZANZIBAR

Office of the Member of Parliament for Colleges and Universities, Zanzibar



The website must use the provided official office logo as the primary brand identity. DO NOT redesign, recreate, distort, recolor, crop, or replace the logo. Preserve the original logo exactly as provided and use it professionally throughout the website.



IMPORTANT:

This is an official public institution website, not a political campaign website. The design must communicate public service, professionalism, accountability, accessibility, education, youth development, representation and transparency. Avoid campaign-style graphics, political slogans, vote-seeking language, party propaganda, exaggerated claims, or promotional political messaging.



DESIGN DIRECTION



Create a premium East African government institutional website inspired by the information architecture and professionalism of official Government of Tanzania and Zanzibar institutional websites.



Visual identity:

- Primary brand color: deep royal/navy blue derived from the official logo.

- Secondary colors: white, light grey and subtle gold accents inspired by the emblem.

- Clean government institutional aesthetic.

- Modern typography.

- Strong visual hierarchy.

- Plenty of white space.

- Professional photography.

- Subtle borders and shadows.

- Avoid excessive gradients.

- Avoid flashy startup-style animations.

- Avoid excessive rounded cards.

- Use the official logo prominently in the header.

- The overall design should feel authoritative but accessible to students and young people.
